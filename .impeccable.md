## Design Context

### Users
OpsTracer serves developers, DevOps engineers, SREs, and engineering administrators who configure on-call coverage and respond to incidents under time pressure.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, operational. The product should feel trustworthy and business-ready without losing the directness engineers expect from technical tools.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the established OpsTracer light palette: lavender as the restrained brand accent, mint for supporting surfaces, warm neutral backgrounds, and semantic red, amber, green, and blue states. Use Host Grotesk for readable business and engineering workflows. Product examples should be buildable components traced to Figma nodes, not abstract placeholder rectangles.

### Design Principles
- Preserve the existing design-system structure and palette.
- Use real component anatomy and source-node references for engineering handoff.
- Tune Host Grotesk by role: line-height 1.04–1.22 for headings, 1.55 for body copy, 1.4 for UI, and 1.4–1.45 for short metadata. Use slightly open tracking for text (`0.006em` body, `0.008em` UI, `0.012em` metadata) and only gently tighten headings (`-0.008em` display, `-0.004em` heading).
- Use predictable fixed type sizes in product UI and fluid display sizes only in marketing compositions.
- Prioritize semantic HTML, keyboard focus, tabular figures, and responsive data access.
