Foundations / Token roles

Color is semantic, not decorative

Each token has a defined job and explicit places where it should not be used.

Brand accent

CTA, banner, footer action, selected state. Avoid large page background fills and body text.

Ink

Headings, body, icons, banner text. Avoid generic surface fills outside planned dark sections.

Muted text

Supporting copy, captions, metadata. Avoid headings and CTA labels.

Border

Dividers, input edges, dropdowns. Avoid text and button fills.

Focus indicator

Keyboard focus rings only. It is not a brand CTA or marketing accent.

Surface

Hero header, card/panel backgrounds, footer reveal. Avoid body text and CTA fills.

# OpsTracer: On-call and Incident Response — Style Reference
> calm-under-pressure, operationally-direct, lavender-accented-dark, high-contrast-section-alternation, product-led

**Theme:** light

## Overview

OpsTracer is an on-call and incident-response platform for teams that need reliable ownership when systems fail. It connects responders, contact methods, schedules, escalation policies, services, and monitoring integrations so an alert reaches the person on call and remains actionable until it is acknowledged or resolved.

The central design tension is urgency versus control. Responders may open the product while tired or under pressure, while Admins need to understand when configuration is saved, publishing, or live. Host Grotesk uses open, readable forms across body, data, navigation, and display roles; paired with the #c190ff lavender accent, near-black #181e15 data surfaces, and restrained status colors, it creates a system that feels calm, explicit, and dependable.

**Voice:** headlines=Direct and outcome-led — "Know who is on call. Handle incidents fast." Short, confident, and specific.; body=Operational and consequence-aware — explain what a setting changes and whether it is saved, publishing, or live.; microcopy=Calm under pressure — use familiar terms such as "Acknowledge incident," "Resolve incident," and "Coverage starts at 18:00." Empty states name the operational consequence, not only the absence.; ctas=Verb-led and explicit — "Create schedule," "Add responder," "Publish changes," and "Acknowledge incident."

## Colors

### Color Philosophy
- Treat surface colors as the atmosphere of a section before applying them as decorative fills. #dceeeb mint anchors the hero gradient and solution cards; #181e15 near-black anchors dark sections. Neither should appear arbitrarily outside their section role.
- Keep foreground tokens role-specific: primary text, secondary text, placeholder text, and inverse text occupy distinct slots. Collapsing them into a single gray scale loses the hierarchy that makes data-dense layouts readable.
### Token Roles
#### Brand & Accent
- **{colors.color.accent-2}** (`#b072ff`): accent-hover. use for hover state of lavender-accent buttons; active state of footer buttons; avoid default/rest state fills; large backgrounds; body text.
- **{colors.color.accent}** (`#c190ff`): brand accent primary. use for CTA buttons; top banner backgrounds; footer buttons; active/selected states; avoid large page background fills; body text on light surfaces; dividers.
#### Text
- **{colors.color.text.muted}** (`#6c6e79`): body-text-secondary. use for secondary/supporting body copy; captions; footer decorative dots; meta text; avoid background fills; headings; CTA labels.
- **{colors.color.ink}** (`#181e15`): foreground. use for headings; body text; icons; top banner text; avoid background fills; button fills; large surface areas.
#### Hairlines & Borders
- **{colors.color.border}** (`#d1d2d5`): border. use for dividers; input borders; profile menu border; dropdown borders; avoid text; icon fills; button backgrounds.
#### Semantic
- **{colors.color.focus-blue}** (`#006dca`): focus-indicator. use for focus ring box-shadow (inset); keyboard focus outlines; skip-to-content link text; avoid decorative fills; button backgrounds; marketing surfaces.
#### Surface
- **{colors.color.surface}** (`#dceeeb`): surface. use for header background (mint variant); footer reveal section background; card/panel backgrounds; avoid body text; CTA button fills; icon fills.

## Typography

### Typography Principles

- Treat typography as a hierarchy of roles within one family: Host Grotesk uses weight 400 for sustained reading, 500 for metadata and compact UI, and 600–650 for navigation, actions, and headings.

- Match type hierarchy to the content voice: Host Grotesk at 32px weight 600 commands a section title; Host Grotesk at 16px weight 400 delivers the explanation beneath it. CTA labels in Host Grotesk weight 600 signal action. Blurring these distinctions flattens the hierarchy the brand relies on to guide the eye.

### Font Family

- **Host Grotesk, Segoe UI, sans-serif** for every product and content role. A single family reduces metric mismatch and keeps engineering handoff predictable.
- Load only weights 400, 500, 600, and 700 with `font-display: swap`.
- Use 16px / 1.55 for reading, 14px / 1.4 for product UI, and 12–13px / 1.4–1.45 only for short metadata.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- All primary and secondary CTA buttons must use border-radius: 100px (fully-pill). Do not apply card radius (5px) or utility radius (4px) to action buttons.
- Always apply #006dca (focus-blue) as an inset box-shadow (3px) on :focus-visible interactive elements. Never suppress focus rings — this is the only accessible focus indicator color in the system.
- Apply 120px top and bottom padding to every major content section. This is the confirmed section rhythm token observed across solutions, operational data, testimonials, and resources sections.
- Apply font-weight 600 to all Host Grotesk navigation items, CTAs, and display headings. Apply font-weight 500 to Host Grotesk supporting labels and banner descriptions. Reserve weight 400 for Host Grotesk body copy only.
- Implement focus-visible states using inset box-shadow rings only — never native outline. Use `inset 0 0 0 3px #006dca` (blue) for general interactive elements and `inset 0 0 0 2px #181e15` (near-black) for footer and banner elements.
- Keep micro-interactions at 0.1s–0.3s. Use 0.1s for transform (icon hover lift), 0.2s for opacity and color changes, and 0.3s for layout-affecting properties (padding, font-size, opacity of panels). The CSS custom property --mp-transition-duration: 200ms and --mp-transition-easing: ease-in-out define the canonical defaults.
- Keep the 32px side inset on all inner content rails. Full-bleed backgrounds span 1440px, but text and components must stay within the 1376px inner rail.
- Maintain 60px button height at desktop and 48px at mobile. These are confirmed computed values; do not reduce below 44px minimum on any viewport.
### Don't
- Do not add decorative drop shadows to buttons or surface cards. The brand explicitly resets box-shadow to none on .srf-btn. Shadows appear only on floating overlays (dropdowns/popovers) at very low opacity (max #0003). Applying shadow to flat surfaces contradicts the restrained, data-forward depth model.
- Do not apply 100px border-radius to cards or containers. Reserve pill radius (100px) exclusively for CTA buttons. Cards use 5px radius.
- Do not apply weight 700 to Host Grotesk headings or nav items as a general style — weight 700 is reserved for inline emphasis within specific contexts (e.g., `<b>` inside browser-warning banners). Heading hierarchy is expressed through size, not by escalating weight beyond 600.
- Do not introduce 'Factor A' or 'Factor A Variable' into UI surfaces without explicit design authorization. These @font-face declarations exist in the stylesheet but have no observed component-level usage — they are likely reserved for a specific product sub-brand or campaign context.
- Do not introduce additional background colors beyond the observed palette (white, #181e15 dark, #dceeeb mint, #e8e1ff lavender gradient, #c190ff accent). Introducing new section backgrounds breaks the deliberate light/dark alternation that structures page rhythm.
- Do not introduce lifestyle or stock photography. Every feature section uses product UI screenshots and operational data as primary media. Photography would weaken the product's emphasis on reliable on-call workflows.
- Do not introduce motion beyond the observed transition set. No keyframe animations were observed outside the global loading spinner (0.75s linear). Do not add entrance animations, scroll-triggered transforms, or parallax effects not present in the evidence.
- Do not mix arbitrary radius values. Marketing cards retain the existing 5px radius; Figma-derived product metric/table components use their evidenced 8px/12px radii; actions use 100px and avatars use 50%.
- Do not reduce section vertical padding below 120px for content sections. Compressing to 60px or 80px collapses the generous whitespace posture that distinguishes this layout from a typical marketing template.
- Do not use #006dca (blue) or #2b94e1 (blue) as a brand accent or CTA color. These tokens exist exclusively as accessibility focus-ring indicators. Using them as primary interactive colors misrepresents the brand palette and undermines the lavender accent system.
