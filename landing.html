Landing page / Scope

A calmer story for high-pressure work.

The landing system now follows the same quiet, spacious rhythm as the refreshed product documentation: large editorial claims, generous pauses, soft product windows, and evidence shown directly on screen.

The Product Design System is the source of truth for colour, typography, spacing, geometry, controls, focus, and iconography. Marketing may increase editorial type and section spacing, but it may not create a second component system.
